# Exploratory Data Analysis Findings

## Key Findings

- Daily NAV generally follows an increasing trend.
- SIP inflows have increased steadily over the observed period.
- Equity-oriented funds receive the largest investments.
- Tier-1 cities contribute the highest number of investors.
- Large fund houses dominate Assets Under Management (AUM).
- Equity folios significantly outnumber debt and hybrid folios.
- Benchmark indices show long-term positive growth.
- Portfolio holdings are concentrated in key sectors.
- Investor demographics indicate diverse participation across age groups and income levels.
- The cleaned datasets are suitable for dashboard creation and advanced business analytics.

## Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis reveals consistent growth in the Indian mutual fund industry. Increasing SIP inflows, expanding investor participation, and rising AUM indicate strong market confidence. The prepared datasets provide a reliable foundation for KPI calculation, dashboard development, and predictive analytics in the subsequent stages of the project.
